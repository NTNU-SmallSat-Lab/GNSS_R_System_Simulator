Scientific assessment
=====================

This page records the code-and-documentation physics audit. It is intended as a concise model-maturity
statement for reviewers and future contributors.

Corrections made by the audit
-----------------------------

.. list-table::
   :widths: 27 35 38
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Finding
     - Consequence
     - Correction
   * - Metre-scale constrained ellipsoid solve accepted a loose vector residual.
     - Some accepted specular points violated equal-angle reflection by nearly a degree.
     - Parameterised the WGS-84 surface by two angles, solved tangent stationarity, and independently
       verify the reflection-vector residual. The acceptance gate is :math:`5\times 10^{-2}` (kept
       deliberately coarse -- see ``TODO.md``); well-posed geometries converge far tighter, and the
       tests pin the angle mismatch below :math:`10^{-7}` deg for converged solutions.
   * - Spherical line of sight sampled only 100 chord points.
     - A sufficiently narrow grazing intersection could be missed.
     - Replaced sampling with the exact closest point on the finite chord.
   * - Ellipsoidal visibility used an equatorial-radius sphere.
     - Valid polar grazing chords could be rejected by a surface up to about 21 km too large.
     - Test the finite chord exactly after scaling WGS-84 to a unit sphere.
   * - Collinear same-side spherical geometry had an undefined plane normal.
     - Valid nadir reflections returned NaN.
     - Added a stable arbitrary plane containing the common radial direction.
   * - BRP always used WGS-84 and accepted any positive line root.
     - Spherical mode was ignored; a root before the receiver could be selected.
     - Use the requested surface and require the intersection to lie beyond the receiver.
   * - Antenna pre-gate used the old azimuth/elevation axis association.
     - Valid looks could be rejected or assigned to the wrong steering axis.
     - Apply elevation to antenna :math:`x` (flight direction) and azimuth to antenna :math:`y`
       consistently in generator, receiver, BRP, configuration and documentation.
   * - Footprint projection used transmitter incidence.
     - Non-specular/backscatter footprint area had the wrong obliquity factor.
     - Project with the receiver scattering angle.
   * - Configured significant wave height was used as RMS height.
     - Rayleigh roughness attenuation was too strong by the :math:`H_s/\sigma_h` conversion.
     - Use :math:`\sigma_h\simeq H_s/4` and expose it in diagnostics.
   * - Scalar glistening area did not preserve the geometric-optics surface integral.
     - At normal incidence with isotropic slopes, the complete diffuse RCS was low by a factor of four.
     - Use :math:`A_\mathrm{eff}=4R_\mathrm{red}^2/P(0,0)` and verify the integrated flat-mirror
       invariant.
   * - Rotated two-scale slope statistics discarded off-diagonal covariance.
     - Intermediate wind-relative azimuths used the wrong facet distribution.
     - Sample the full rotated covariance through a Cholesky factor.
   * - Delay-Doppler gradient treated the Earth surface as stationary in ECI.
     - Doppler and cell area omitted rigid Earth rotation.
     - Include :math:`\mathbf{v}_s=\boldsymbol{\omega}_E\times\mathbf{r}` and its spatial derivative.
   * - Coverage segmentation included invalid separators and dropped valid endpoints.
     - Short visits and the first sample after a gap could be lost.
     - Split exact contiguous valid-index runs; retain singleton segments and support both grid types.
   * - Moving-target visibility prefilter imposed a hard-coded 40 deg elevation.
     - Geometrically reachable antenna looks were discarded before the antenna gate.
     - Use the physical horizon (0 deg) for the coarse prefilter and apply steering limits later.
   * - Shore buffer metres were added directly to longitude/latitude degrees.
     - Land endpoints could be displaced by thousands of degrees.
     - Convert the local direction to east/north metres before applying the buffer.
   * - Route caches ignored mesh-generation settings.
     - A changed grid, connection radius or water buffer could silently reuse a stale route.
     - Compare every route- and sampling-affecting setting in cache metadata.
   * - ``write_tle_file`` assigned a path but did not write the generated TLE.
     - ``tle_exists`` could be true for a nonexistent file and ``set_epoch`` could fail while loading it.
     - Write the TLE to its deterministic autogenerated path and test that Skyfield can load it.
   * - Multi-record TLE selection used different records for metadata and Skyfield propagation.
     - Direct index or name selection could label one satellite while propagating another.
     - Resolve one selected record index and use it for both parsed metadata and the Skyfield object.
   * - Classical orbit inputs lacked physical range checks.
     - Invalid eccentricity, inclination, perigee or epoch could reach the nonlinear TLE fit.
     - Validate finite elements, bound :math:`i` and :math:`e`, require perigee above WGS-84, and require
       a UTC-aware epoch.
   * - Future/offline coordinate transforms failed solely on Astropy's IERS age limit.
     - Reproducible future-date runs could abort despite installed Earth-orientation data.
     - Permit the installed table as an offline fallback and document the resulting accuracy limitation.
   * - Directivity integrated the isotropic-element mirror back lobe over the full sphere.
     - Every gain, :math:`C/N_0`, SNR and SINR was 3.0 dB pessimistic; footprint areas doubled.
     - Integrate the pattern power over the front (shielded-panel) hemisphere only; anchored by
       closed-form directivity tests (uniform pattern :math:`D=2`, :math:`\cos^2\theta` pattern
       :math:`D=6`).
   * - The geometric-optics facet-slope PDF used the total optical Cox-Munk variance.
     - Sub-wavelength ripples were counted as L-band mirrors: the glint-peak NRCS was ~2.8 dB low and
       the off-specular GO backscatter skirt ~13 dB high at 20 deg incidence.
     - Use the large-scale (:math:`k < k_\mathrm{radar}/3`) slope variances from the Elfouhaily
       spectrum with the Cox-Munk anisotropy split -- the same two-scale division the Bragg tilt
       average already used.
   * - The coherent specular term used flat-Earth image theory.
     - Calm-sea coherent power was ~1.2--1.8 dB optimistic (no convex-surface defocus).
     - Apply the spherical-Earth divergence :math:`D^2` (Coddington astigmatic mirror radii), exposed
       as a diagnostic and anchored to closed form.
   * - The Elfouhaily long-wave equilibrium parameter used exponent 0.55.
     - Silent deviation from the cited Eq. 34 (up to ~8% in :math:`\alpha_p` at :math:`\Omega_c = 5`).
     - Use :math:`\alpha_p = 6\times10^{-3}\sqrt{\Omega_c}`; the deliberate :math:`L_\mathrm{PM}`
       regularisation of :math:`B_\mathrm{high}` is now documented in-code.
   * - Two-scale Bragg below ~20 deg incidence is dominated by the near-nadir
       :math:`1/\sin^4\theta` divergence leaking through tilted facets (diagnosed, not yet re-modelled).
     - Lowering the empirical 20 deg gate would inject tens of dB of spurious clutter.
     - Documented the gate as divergence protection; the facet-local
       :math:`k_B(\theta_\mathrm{local}) \ge k_\mathrm{radar}/3` gate is recorded in ``TODO.md`` as the
       principled refinement.

Model maturity and suitable use
-------------------------------

The corrected code is suitable for geometry studies, parameter sweeps, algorithm development and
relative comparisons within a clearly fixed scenario. The specular/BRP geometry and first-order
delay-Doppler derivatives now have strong numerical verification.

The code should not yet be used without further validation for absolute ship-detection range,
probability of detection, mission requirements closure or geophysical retrieval accuracy. The dominant
remaining uncertainties are the uncalibrated ship RCS, ideal array-factor antenna, assumed GNSS EIRP,
empirical ocean-model composition, first-order specular DDM singularity, footprint-only candidate
association, and absence of detector/speckle statistics. See :doc:`validation` for the evidence hierarchy
and required external-validation programme.
